# Story

**Point:** a video is six small steps, and each one leaves a file behind.

1. **Brief, then facts** (Reference, Data): the spec and the sourced numbers come first, as spec.md and data.json.
2. **Shape, then build** (Story, Motion): the point and beats become story.md, then the composition index.html.
3. **Time, then deliver** (Direct, Ship): timing.json fixes every window, and final.mp4 is the result.
